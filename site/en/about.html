About XLANORA

XLANORA is a marketplace that connects clients with independent freelancers. Good work should be easy to find, easy to book and easy to trust.

How XLANORA works

Clients search for a service, compare freelancers and their portfolios, and send a booking request. The freelancer accepts, you agree on the price and delivery time, and you work together directly.

Direct payments

XLANORA does not hold, collect or transfer client money. Agree on a payment method with your freelancer outside the platform.

Important to know

  • XLANORA connects clients with independent freelancers. Payments are arranged directly between clients and freelancers. XLANORA does not charge service commissions or process payments.
  • Agree on the price and the delivery time before starting, and confirm the requirements in writing.
  • Keep copies of agreements and receipts. Be careful with unknown payment links and unexpected advance fees.
